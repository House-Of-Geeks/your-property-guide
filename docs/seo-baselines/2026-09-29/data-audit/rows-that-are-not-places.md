## Deleting them

Not done. The redirects and filters make the rows inert, so deleting is housekeeping, not a fix. It is a hard delete
in production and is left for Jos to run. If wanted, in one transaction, after a fresh export of the 31 rows:

1. Re-point the 82 property addresses: `UPDATE "PropertyAddress" SET "suburbSlug" = 'pipalyatjara-sa-0872' WHERE "suburbSlug" = 'pipalyatjara-sa-872';`
2. Re-point or remove the 30 crime rows and the climate row (`"SuburbCrimeStat"`, `"SuburbClimate"`, by `"suburbSlug"`). The crime rows
   describe incidents at interstate addresses, so they belong to no South Australian suburb: set `"suburbSlug"` to NULL.
3. Remove the slugs from the `nearbySuburbs` arrays of the 8 suburbs that list them (the site already filters them when it reads).
4. `DELETE FROM "Suburb" WHERE slug IN (...the 31...)`. No school, listing, package or deal refers to any of them, so nothing cascades.
5. Re-run `npx tsx scripts/seo/non-localities.ts`: the 31 leave the list, and the redirects should then be kept by hand in
   `next.config.ts` for as long as the old URLs are worth catching.

Step 5 is the reason to leave the rows where they are: while they exist, the generator keeps their redirects without anyone remembering to.
