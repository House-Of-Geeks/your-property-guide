# Help to Buy: official facts, checked 6 to 7 October 2026

Sources are official only: firsthomebuyers.gov.au (Housing Australia), legislation.gov.au, housingaustralia.gov.au media releases, budget.gov.au, state government sites, and participating lenders' own pages for lender facts. Figures are copied exactly; wording is paraphrased. Bracketed numbers point to the source list at the end.

## 1. Status and dates

- **Applications opened 5 December 2025** in NSW, Vic, Qld, SA, the ACT and the NT [12][13].
- **Other states joined later:**
  - WA from 22 December 2025 [14].
  - Tasmania passed its law on 22 May 2026 and joined from 9 June 2026, making the scheme national [16][17][24].
- **Open now.** On 1 July 2026 Housing Australia announced 10,000 new places for 2026–27 [17].
- **Places** [9 s48–51]:
  - 10,000 a year, with unused places carried over.
  - Used places are capped at 40,000 in total.
  - Places are split between states by population. After 1 March each year, any uncommitted places can be used in any state.
- **Waitlist or ballot:** not published. The lender tells you "if a place is available" [7].
- **Changes since launch:**
  - 20 Feb 2026: review rights at the Administrative Review Tribunal [11].
  - 1 Jul 2026: income limits rose from $100k/$160k to **$103k/$165k**, and the renovation threshold rose from $20k to $21k [2][17].
  - 27 Jul 2026: Teachers Mutual Bank Limited joined [18].
  - 6 Oct 2026: Queensland Country Bank joined [19].
  - The 2026–27 Budget made no change to Help to Buy [21][22].
  - Price caps are unchanged since 13 June 2025 and are not indexed [9][2].

## 2. Government contribution

- **Share of the price:** 5% to 30% for an existing home, 5% to 40% for a new home [9 s16].
- **No LMI:** the contribution plus the buyer's deposit must reach at least 20% of the value, so no lenders mortgage insurance is charged [9 s16(2)][5 p6].
- **Deposit:** at least 2%. The buyer must also put in the most they can reasonably afford, as the lender assesses it [4][5 p11][9 s17(f)].
- **No rent or interest** on the government's share [4]. The contribution is secured by an interest-free loan [5 p5].
- **Fees:**
  - Housing Australia charges no application or approval fees [9 s57].
  - The buyer pays stamp duty, legal costs, mortgage registration (including the government's second mortgage), and a valuation each time they make a repayment [5 p6, p11, p39].

## 3. Eligibility

- **Age and citizenship:** 18 or over, and an Australian citizen. Permanent residents are not eligible [1][3][9 s17(a)].
- **Income limits for 2026–27** [2][3][9 s19]:

  | Applicant | Taxable income limit |
  |---|---|
  | Single | $103,000 |
  | Joint (couples) | $165,000 |
  | Single parent | $165,000 |

  - Tested on the most recent ATO Notice of Assessment.
  - No exceptions for bonuses.
  - Indexed to wages each 1 July.
- **Applicants:** two at most [3].
- **Property ownership:**
  - You can't own property in Australia or overseas when you apply. Previous owners are eligible [1][8].
  - Single parents have exceptions [5 p17][9 s18].
- **Living in the home:** move in at settlement for an established home, or within 3 months of completion for a new build [3][9 s31].
- **No dollar asset test,** but there is a financial capacity test: if you could buy without the scheme, you're ineligible [9 s20][5 p16].
- **The loan:** one principal-and-interest loan from a participating lender, up to 30 years [9 s22].

## 5. Living with it

- **Buying back the government's share:**
  - Each repayment must be at least 5% of the home's current value, or the whole remaining share.
  - The value is set by a valuation the owner pays for [9 s43][5 p39].
- **If your income rises:**
  - Income is reviewed at least every 5 years [9 s34][5 p32].
  - Only being over the limit for 2 years in a row triggers anything [9 s36–37].
  - If the lender finds you can afford it, you pay at least 5% within 90 days. If not, it's reassessed in 12 months or more [9 s37].
  - You never have to repay if it would push you into LMI [9 s37(7)][5 p33].
- **Selling:**
  - You can sell at any time, but must notify Housing Australia, and the sale must be at arm's length [9 s44].
  - The government gets its percentage of the sale price or a valuation, whichever is higher [5 p40].
  - The proceeds pay the bank first, then the government's share, and you keep the rest [5 p40].
- **Renovations:**
  - Notify Housing Australia before work of $21,000 or more in 12 months, or any work that needs council approval [2][5 p35].
  - The government's share is then adjusted so you keep the value you add. If you don't notify, there's no adjustment [5 p35].
- **Renting out:**
  - Not allowed, and no business use [9 Sch1 cl1.9–1.10].
  - Exceptions: ADF postings, an employer relocation after 12 months in the job (up to 12 months plus two 12-month extensions), and serious illness or compassionate grounds [9 s30].
- **Death:**
  - Up to two eligible beneficiaries can take over. Otherwise the government's share is recovered, usually within 2 years [5 p42].
  - A surviving joint tenant continues, and must notify Housing Australia within 90 days [5 p42].
- **Separation:**
  - A participant can be removed only if the lender confirms the remaining person can manage the loan, or under a court order. At least one original participant must stay [9 s38][5 p38].
- **Refinancing:**
  - To another participating lender: allowed, with a part or full buy-back.
  - To any other lender: the government's share must be repaid in full.
  - Extra borrowing is allowed only to reduce the government's share, for repairs or improvements, or in hardship [5 p41][9 s32].

## 6. How to apply

1. Apply through a participating lender only (brokers can submit). You can't apply to Housing Australia directly [1][3].
2. The lender's conditional approval reserves a place for 90 days, with one 90-day extension possible [1][5 p26].
3. The approval letter states your maximum purchase price, and you get a letter of support to show sellers [5 p27].
4. The contract must allow at least 30 days to settlement, and the lender needs everything 29 days before settlement [5 p27].
5. You must settle within 90 days of final approval [3].
6. Housing Australia's conveyancer handles the Participation Agreement and the second mortgage [1][5 p29].

**Lenders (official list [7]):**
- Bank Australia, since launch.
- Commonwealth Bank, since launch. Its own FAQ is out of date and lists only two lenders.
- Teachers Mutual Bank Limited (Teachers Mutual Bank, Health Professionals Bank, Firefighters Mutual Bank, UniBank), from 27 July 2026, with brokers from 6 October 2026.
- Queensland Country Bank, from 6 October 2026.

## 7. Combining with other schemes

- **Not allowed with:** the Commonwealth home guarantees (so not with the 5% Deposit Scheme), any other shared equity scheme, or state home-ownership loans and guarantees [9 s17(d), Sch1 cl1.6][5 p9].
- **Allowed with:** first home owner grants, stamp duty concessions and the First Home Super Saver Scheme [9 s17 note][3][5 p12].

**State schemes:**
- **NSW Shared Equity Home Buyer Helper:** closed 30 June 2024 [29].
- **Victorian Homebuyer Fund:** closed to new applications 10 September 2025 [31][32].
- **Qld Boost to Buy:** open [33].
  - Round 2 has up to 500 places, regional only (South East Queensland appointments are all taken).
  - $1m price cap, 2% deposit, up to 30% for new homes and 25% for existing.
  - Income limits of $155k single and $232k for couples or applicants with dependants.
  - Excludes anyone receiving Commonwealth shared equity.
- **WA Keystart:** SharedStart, GoodStart and Sole Parent loans [34].
  - Urban Connect Shared Equity (launched 8 Oct 2025) covers new apartments and townhouses up to $730k, with a share up to 35% or $250k.
- **SA HomeStart Shared Equity Option:** a 5–25% share, net household income up to $120k, $750k price cap [35].
- **Tas MyHome:** open through Bank of us [36].
  - Up to 40% or $300k for new homes, up to 30% or $150k for existing.
  - Price caps of $800k (new) and $750k (existing).

## 8. Uptake

- **10 Feb 2026:** more than 2,356 places approved and 278 households in their homes. Median deposit $29,000 [15].
- **1 Jul 2026:** more than 7,200 applications, of which 4,800 have found a home or settled [17].
  - Demand is strongest in Vic, then NSW, then Qld.
  - About 7 in 10 applicants are single, 12% are single parents and 86% are first home buyers.
  - Median deposit $30,000.
